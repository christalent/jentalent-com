Notarization Types

🖊️ Acknowledgment

The signer personally appears, is identified, and acknowledges that they signed the document voluntarily.

✍️ Jurat / Subscribe & Swear

The signer personally appears, is identified, and swears or affirms that the contents of the document are true.

✅ Copy Certification

I certify that a copy of a document is a true and accurate copy of the original.

📱 Remote Online Notarization (RON)

Available upon request — documents notarized via secure video conference (where permitted by law).

Identification Requirements

Per Washington State notary regulations, signers must provide adequate identification. Acceptable forms include:

🪪 Passport

Valid U.S. or foreign passport.

🚗 Driver's License

Valid state-issued driver's license or ID card.

👥 Credible Witness

A credible witness who personally knows the signer may be used when the signer lacks acceptable ID.

🤝 Personally Known

If I already know the signer and can confirm their identity, no additional ID is required.
